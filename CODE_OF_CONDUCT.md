# Code of Conduct

Keep discussions useful, respectful, and relevant to the repository.

- Discuss the work and evidence without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect creators' attribution and requests to correct or remove material.
- Do not post spam, private information, credentials, or misleading source claims.
- Accept constructive corrections and explain disagreements with evidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may limit participation after repeated abuse. Use GitHub's content-reporting controls for abuse or contact a repository maintainer through the contact information on their GitHub profile.
